How to add this to Gmail
  1. Select everything in the white box below (click inside it, then press ⌘A) and copy it (⌘C).
  2. In Gmail, open Settings → See all settings → General → Signature, and create a new one.
  3. Paste into the signature box (⌘V), then choose it as the default for new emails and replies, and save.
