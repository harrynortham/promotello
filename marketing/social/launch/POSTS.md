# Launch pack: social posts for business owners

Images are in `out/`. To change wording or design, edit `build.mjs` and run
`node marketing/social/launch/build.mjs` (add a prefix like `05` to render
only those).

| Size | Files | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1080×1350 | `*-feed.png`, carousels `02-*`, `04-*` | Instagram and Facebook feed (also LinkedIn) |
| 1600×900 | `*-wide.png` | X, Facebook link-style posts |
| 1080×1920 | `*-story.png`, `06-video-*-cover.png` | Stories, and covers for TikTok, Reels and Shorts |
| 1280×720 | `06-youtube-thumbnail.png` | YouTube thumbnail |
| Profile | `07-avatar.png`, `07-cover-*.png`, `08-highlight-*.png` | Profile picture on every platform; X, Facebook and YouTube headers |

Link everywhere: **promotello.com/business**. On Instagram and TikTok, put
it in the bio and say "link in bio".

Before posting the founding member post, check how many of the 50 places are
left on /business/pricing, and that the photos' licence covers social use.

## Short videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

Film vertically on a phone, 15 to 40 seconds, with on-screen text, since most
people watch muted. Use the cover images as the thumbnail. Captions are below
each script; the TikTok one can also go on Reels and Shorts.

### Video 1: Paper vs phone (cover `06-video-1-cover.png`)

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | A soggy paper stamp card pulled out of a jeans pocket or a washing machine | "Your stamp cards are in the wash." |
| 2–6s | Hand rummaging through a wallet full of crumpled cards | "And the rest are at home in a drawer." |
| 6–12s | A phone with the Promotello loyalty card, a staff phone scanning it, a stamp appears | "This one lives on their phone." |
| 12–16s | Close-up of the stamps filling up | "Staff stamp it by scanning. Nobody can stamp their own." |
| 16–20s | Screen showing promotello.com/business | "Free to start. Link in bio." |

Caption: "RIP paper stamp cards 🪦 #smallbusiness #cafeowner #loyaltycard #shoplocal"

### Video 2: A digital loyalty card in 15 minutes (cover `06-video-2-cover.png`)

A screen recording of signing up a real (or test) business, sped up, with a
timer in the corner.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Timer at 00:00, the signup page | "Setting up a loyalty card. Timer's on." |
| 2–10s | Business details, address, hours (sped up) | "Business details" |
| 10–18s | Creating the loyalty card: 9 stamps, free coffee | "The reward: a free coffee after 9" |
| 18–25s | Downloading the QR poster, then the printed poster on a window | "Print the poster" |
| 25–32s | A customer scanning it and joining the card | "Customers scan and join. No app." |
| 32–38s | Timer stops under 15:00 | "Done. Free to start, link in bio." |

Caption: "Digital loyalty card, set up from scratch ⏱️ #smallbusinesstips #loyaltyprogram #cafe"

Make a **YouTube version** (3 to 6 minutes, thumbnail `06-youtube-thumbnail.png`) from the same recording at normal speed, with a voiceover:
- Title: "How to set up a digital loyalty card for your café in 15 minutes"
- Chapters: 0:00 Why paper cards fail · 0:40 Sign up · 1:30 Create the loyalty card · 2:30 Print your QR poster · 3:15 What customers see · 4:00 What staff do at the till · 4:45 Pricing
- Description: start with "Set up a free digital loyalty card for your café, barber, salon or shop in about 15 minutes with Promotello. No app for customers, no card reader. Start free: https://www.promotello.com/business", followed by the chapters.
