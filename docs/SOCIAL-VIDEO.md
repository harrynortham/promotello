# Making videos for social

Moved out of the README, which is for how the app is built.

A simple workflow:

1. **Idea and script.** Ask an assistant for hooks and a 30–60 second script.
   The first 2 seconds matter most.
2. **Visuals.** Film yourself, generate clips (Veo, Kling or Runway), or use an
   avatar.
3. **Voice.** Your own, or an AI voice from ElevenLabs.
4. **Edit.** CapCut is free and built for TikTok and Reels. Add auto-captions,
   since most people watch muted, plus trending audio.
5. **Post natively** to each platform. TikTok, Instagram and YouTube all ask you
   to label realistic AI-generated content, so turn on the AI label when it
   applies.

Who to write to and what to say is in [OUTREACH.md](OUTREACH.md).
